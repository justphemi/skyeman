How it works

From couch to canopy in five steps.

Here's exactly what happens between you and a confirmed jump.

Step 1

Find a slot

Browse upcoming dates across Ikoyi, Lekki and Victoria Island. Each slot shows live capacity and your instructor.

Step 2

Pick a package

Tandem for first-timers, AFF if you're chasing a license, Solo if you're already certified, or Group for a big day out.

Step 3

Confirm & pay

Pay securely — simulated in this demo — and your slot is locked in. You'll see the booking in your dashboard immediately.

Step 4

Jump day

Arrive 30 minutes early. Brief, gear up, board the plane. Freefall. Canopy. Land. Take lots of photos.

Step 5

Reschedule or cancel

Plans change. Move your slot for free up to the day before, or cancel for a full refund. No admin emails required.
