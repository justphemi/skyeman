About Skyeman

Skydiving, made simple.

We're Lagos' friendliest tandem skydiving operator — three drop zones, certified instructors, no paperwork, no stress.

Certified

Real instructors

Every tandem jump is attached to a USPA-rated instructor with hundreds — often thousands — of jumps behind them.

Safety

Top-grade gear

We use current, regularly-inspected parachutes, harnesses, and aircraft. Your safety is the only thing we don't compromise on.

Lagos

Three locations

Ikoyi, Lekki and Victoria Island — pick whichever is closest. We bring the plane, the gear, and the calm nerves.

Our story

Built for first-timers, loved by repeat jumpers

Skyeman started with one tandem instructor, one Cessna, and a single drop zone in Ikoyi. Years later we still hold on to that original idea: skydiving should be approachable, transparent, and — most importantly — fun.

Today we operate three locations across Lagos with eight instructors between them. Whether it's your first jump or your hundredth, we want you to walk off the aircraft with a story worth telling.
