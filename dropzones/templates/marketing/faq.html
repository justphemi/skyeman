FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the most common things people ask before booking.

Do I need experience to tandem skydive?

No. Tandem jumps are designed for first-timers — you're attached to a certified instructor the whole time.

What's the minimum age?

18 for tandem and AFF. 16 for Solo with parental consent. Under-18s need a parent or guardian present.

Is there a weight limit?

Yes — it varies by package. Tandem is up to 100 kg, AFF up to 95 kg. The booking form enforces this for you.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel any time before the jump for a full refund. Use your dashboard — no emails, no phone calls.

Can I reschedule to a different day?

Yes. Pick any open slot at the same drop zone, up to the day before your jump. Free, no questions asked.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothes and trainers. We supply the jumpsuit, harness, goggles, and gloves.

What happens if the weather is bad?

We cancel the slot and you'll be notified. Your booking moves to "weather cancelled" and you can rebook for free.

Are the payments real?

No — this is a university project. All payments are simulated. No real card details are collected or stored.

Still have questions?

Our AI assistant is ready to help, or you can reach out directly.
